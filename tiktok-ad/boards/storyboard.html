Hunu TikTok ad: storyboard

You already have a price in front of you. Before you pay, open Hunu and it tells you, cheekily, whether you can do better. Eight frames, about 23 seconds, vertical.

  • Format 1080 × 1920, 30 fps
  • Length about 23 s
  • Voice cheeky, no voiceover, big on-screen lines
  • Sound music bed plus a few effects on clicks and landings
  • Look Hunu's own yellow, ink, type (Hind) and UI

Every number is real: one run of Hunu's own "Rank this deal" flow on 14 Nov 2026 for a $9.97 ESP32 development board page, buyer in Accra. The verdict wording is Hunu's own. Shipping amounts are Hunu's estimates. The shop page in frame 1 is a neutral stand-in, not a real store's design.

Act 1: The price in front of you
01: Step away
0.0 to 2.6s

A shop page, $9.97, the cursor a hair from "Add to cart". It freezes.

Step away from the checkout button

Sound: near silence, one record-scratch stop.

02: Second opinion
2.6 to 5.0s

The Hunu panel docks. It has already spotted the price on the page. The cursor clicks "Rank this deal".

Get a second opinion first

Sound: music enters; click.

Act 2: The verdict and the comparison
03: The verdict
5.0 to 8.2s

The button becomes the verdict: 72% counts up, #6 of 26, then Hunu's own line and the 62% that stings.

Hunu has opinions

Sound: flap ticks under the count; pop on the highlight.

04: The other tab
8.2 to 12.2s

This page against two better deals, row by row: price, shipping, then total landed cost. GH₵399 against GH₵153.

The grass is greener on the other tab

Sound: a tick per row; the music lifts on landed cost.

05: Shows its working
12.2 to 15.0s

Punch in on the value scores. "Best value" lands on 87, and the four reasons behind it fill in.

Yes, it shows its working

Sound: pop on the badge.

Act 3: The payoff and the sign-off
06: The best deal
15.0 to 17.8s

The winning column grows into the rank 1 card: GH₵153 delivered. The cursor clicks "Go to offer".

Now you may check out

Sound: riser into the click, once.

07: Hunu it
17.8 to 20.2s

Hard cut on the click. Three lines land one at a time, on the beat.

Cute product. Ugly price. Hunu it.

Sound: one hit per line.

08: End card
20.2 to 23.0s

The black "Hunu it." block grows to fill the frame; the mark lands, then the last line and the call to action hold.

Your wallet called. It said Hunu.

Sound: music resolves and fades.

Open points

  • Store names. The frames show amazon.com, Tonaton and Jiji Ghana because that is what Hunu returned. They can be swapped for neutral labels ("This page", "Seller in Kumasi") if you would rather not name stores in an ad.
  • Call to action. "Add Hunu to your browser" assumes a public install link. If Hunu is not in a store yet, the button needs a different line.
  • Product. The example is an ESP32 development board. Any product can be swapped in by running the same flow.
